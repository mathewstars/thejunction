# Lanvi submission film, scenario v5 (about 1:45, hackathon video, limit 2:00)

Context: Junction hackathon, challenge by Aalto University "Beyond the Clinic" (research track), plus Google's "Best use of Gemini API". Product: Lanvi, an iPhone app for people who have finished TMS treatment for depression. In the app the person looks after an Arctic fox (short focus game, one minute of breathing, three good things at night). The phone reads steps and sleep from Apple Health and, during the game and breathing, how the face moves (ARKit numbers, no picture leaves the phone). A server compares each person only with their own normal, Gemini reads the raw face numbers and code checks every item it reports, a clinician page shows it, and an urgent note ("I need help") alerts the clinic the same day. All patient data in the demo is example data; nothing has been tested with patients. The team wants to propose a study to Aalto: about 30 people after TMS, 12 weeks, each compared with themselves.

One deep male voice over soft piano. Cuts every 2 to 3 seconds, on the beat. Generated footage (photoreal woman seen from behind, white Arctic fox cub) is labelled "Illustrative footage, generated"; charts and screens are real and labelled "Example data".
