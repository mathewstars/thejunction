# AstraZeneca challenge: Equal Care Across Finland

Text of the challenge page on the Junction platform, taken on 13 Nov 2026. The page export itself is not in the repo because it carries personal contact details.

## The task

Help ensure equitable and effective cancer care for everyone in Finland: time to diagnosis is optimal and patients start treatment within target timeframes, wherever they live.

Build a nationwide, AI-powered system that:

1. **Identifies the gap.** Detects differences in access to cancer care or in treatment outcomes.
2. **Locates the gap in the care pathway.** Shows where the difference emerges: detection, referral from primary to specialist care, diagnosis, or treatment initiation.
3. **Recommends what to do.** The most important part. Concrete, evidence-based improvement proposals that could be implemented at national or county level. Diagnosing the problem is not enough.

Formats they name as possible: an AI analyst that explains indicator trends and proposes actions, a monitoring dashboard with recommendations, an early-warning system for emerging delays, a simulation of the impact of an intervention.

## Minimum requirements

- **Reliable evidence.** Public data and/or insights from AstraZeneca mentors. Show the sources supporting every claim.
- **Goal 3.** Cover at least one indicator connected to Goal 3 of the national cancer strategy.
- **Actionable recommendations.** Concrete proposals, not only an analysis.
- **A working demo.** Slides alone are not sufficient.

## Judging

| Criterion | Weight | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Actionability | 30% | Are the proposals concrete enough for a health center or wellbeing services county to implement? |
| Insight | 25% | Does the solution reveal a real and meaningful gap, and show where it emerges in the care pathway? |
| Data quality and transparency | 20% | Are the sources clear, limitations acknowledged, findings free from unsupported assumptions? |
| Execution | 15% | Does it work in a live demonstration in front of the judges? |
| Clarity | 10% | Can a decision-maker understand the key message and proposed action within two minutes? |

Prizes: 1000 €, 750 €, 250 €. AstraZeneca would like to continue with promising solutions after Junction, ideally with a Finnish wellbeing services county.

## Known bottlenecks (their starting points)

- Late detection.
- The transition from primary to specialist care: referrals delayed or not progressing.
- Time from diagnosis to treatment: patients not starting within the recommended target timeframes.

Data note from the page: data on cancer care in Finland is limited, and addressing that gap is part of the challenge. Public, aggregate data only; no patient-level data is provided or needed.

## Goal 3 of the national cancer strategy 2026–2035

"Varmistetaan yhdenvertainen ja vaikuttava syövänhoito" (ensure equal and effective cancer care). Our translation from `Kansallinen_syopastrategia_2026_2035_FINAL.pdf`; the Finnish text is the record.

Headline indicators:

- Five-year survival of poor-prognosis cancers reaches the Nordic level. Footnote: relative survival rises from the 2023 NORDCAN level by more than 14 percentage points in lung cancer, 6 in pancreatic cancer and 14 in liver cancer; Nordic level means the average of Sweden and Norway.
- Cancer mortality falls by 10% (now 13 300 people a year).
- Patients get to treatment within the target times. Footnote: THL, Ohjaus 21/2016, "Hoitoonpääsyn seuranta syövän hoidossa".

Sub-goals: 3.1 national treatment guidelines and care pathways, 3.2 a national cancer care quality register with peer review, 3.3 rehabilitation, psychosocial support and palliative care in the pathway, 3.4 functional-capacity data guiding care of older patients, 3.5 enough skilled staff.

Useful statements in section 3.1 and 3.2: Finland has no national system that coordinates cancer-specific guidelines, pathways and their indicators (including access to treatment); no cancer care quality register exists yet; comparison between regions is occasional and based on coarse indicators.

Cross-cutting equality indicator: regional and socioeconomic differences in incidence, care and mortality are available to the services and steer them.

Neighbouring Goal 2 indicators that describe the detection end of the pathway: 80% participation in every screening programme in every wellbeing services county; at least 70% of cancers diagnosed at stage I–II.
